For reflection and entertainment

Tarot in Intuo is for reflection and entertainment only. The cards are shuffled at random on your phone, and the meanings are general prompts for thinking, not statements about you or your future. Intuo cannot predict events, and nothing in it is medical, psychological, legal, financial or other professional advice. Please don't make important decisions based on a reading, and talk to a qualified professional about health, legal, money or safety matters. You must be at least 13 years old to use Intuo.
