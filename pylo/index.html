About Pylo

A clean SSH client and SFTP file browser for the servers you look after: saved hosts, a terminal with a key bar for Esc, Tab, Ctrl and the arrows, Ed25519 and RSA keys made or imported on your phone, snippets, port forwarding and App Lock. Passwords and private keys stay on your phone, encrypted by Android's Keystore; every server's host key is checked; there is no account and no cloud. A built-in Demo Server lets you try it offline.
