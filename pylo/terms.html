Using Pylo

Pylo is provided by Aplogon, an independent app developer based in Sri Lanka. It is an SSH client: it connects your phone to servers you add, gives you a terminal on them, lets you browse and move their files over SFTP, makes and stores SSH keys, and forwards ports through them. By using the app you agree to these terms. You must be at least 16 years old to use Pylo.

Your servers and accounts

Use Pylo only with servers and accounts that you own or are allowed to use, and follow the rules of whoever runs them. You are responsible for what you do on your servers through Pylo, for the commands you run and the files you change or delete, and for keeping your passwords, keys and phone safe. Commands run on a server act on that server straight away and often cannot be undone, so take care, and keep backups of anything important.

Pylo checks each server's host key and warns you when it changes; trust a new or changed key only when you know why it is new. Older algorithms, when you turn them on for a host, are weaker; use them only for devices that need them.

What Pylo does not do

Pylo 1.0 does not support Mosh, jump hosts, agent forwarding, X11 or remote (-R) port forwarding, and it does not sync or back up your hosts, keys or passwords between devices: if you lose or reset your phone, they are gone, so keep your own copies of important keys. Android may close a session in the background when it needs memory. The terminal follows the common xterm standard, but some programs may display differently from a desktop terminal.
