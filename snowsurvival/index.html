About Snow Survival

A cozy idle arcade game with no fighting and no enemies: serve cheerful customers, collect cash and bring six snowy islands back to life, one stall at a time. It is free to play and in-app purchases are optional. There is no account and no sign-in: your progress is saved on your phone. On iPhone the game has no ads. On Android it can show short opt-in videos and one small banner, from ad networks set up for children's apps, under child-directed settings and without the advertising ID; no ad network is started in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or Brazil.
