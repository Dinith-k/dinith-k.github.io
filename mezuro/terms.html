Mezuro is provided by Aplogon, an independent app developer based in Sri Lanka. It offers construction calculators that estimate the materials for concrete, masonry, walls, floors, roofs, landscaping and wood framing, a list of what to buy with your own prices, and projects you can share as estimates. By using the app you agree to these terms. You must be at least 16 years old to use Mezuro.

Results are estimates

Mezuro works out quantities from the measurements you enter, with the formulas and typical figures it shows under How It's Worked Out on every calculator (for example bag yields, densities and coverage rates). Its results are estimates for planning and pricing a job. Real jobs vary with the site, the materials, cutting, breakage and how the work is done, so check quantities with your supplier before you buy, and add a sensible allowance for waste.

Mezuro is not engineering, structural or architectural advice, does not check anything against a building code or standard, and does not certify or approve any design. For footings, foundations, reinforcement, roofs, stairs, retaining walls and anything else that carries a load or affects safety, follow your local building rules and ask a qualified professional. You are responsible for how you use Mezuro's results.

Prices in Mezuro are the ones you enter. Mezuro does not know or suggest market prices, and an estimate you share is your own estimate, not an offer or a quote from Aplogon.
