What a stamp is, and isn't

A stamp records what your phone reported when you took the photo: the position and its accuracy from the phone's location services, the address from the phone's address lookup, and the time from the phone's clock. Marcato never changes or invents a position, and it says on the stamp when a position is approximate, old or from a mock-location app. But positions can be off (indoors, near tall buildings, with approximate location), addresses are the nearest ones known, not a survey, and a phone's clock can be set by its owner. A stamp is a record you made, not a certified, legally verified or tamper-proof one. Check whether it meets the requirements of whoever you send it to.

Use Marcato lawfully. Respect people's privacy: don't photograph or share pictures of people or private places where you are not allowed to, and remember that a photo's stamp, and its file if you keep the location in it, shows where it was taken.
