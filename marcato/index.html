About Marcato

A GPS map camera for proof of work: every photo is stamped with a street map, the address, the coordinates, the date and time and your notes, with the location's accuracy shown honestly. Templates for site visits, deliveries, inspections and surveys, jobs with numbered photos, and PDF reports with a map. Your location is used only while the camera is open and is never faked; photos are saved in your gallery, and there is no account.
