About Daktylo

Honest typing speed tests and touch-typing practice: net and gross WPM and accuracy measured the standard way, with autocorrect and suggestions switched off, a 27-lesson course from the home row to numbers, weak-key practice, a key heat map, stats, streaks and a daily goal. Works with your phone's keyboard or a USB or Bluetooth keyboard. No account: your results stay on your phone.
