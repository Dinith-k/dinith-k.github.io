Using Daktylo

Daktylo is provided by Aplogon, an independent app developer based in Sri Lanka. It offers typing speed tests, touch-typing lessons, practice with your own texts, statistics and a daily practice reminder. By using the app you agree to these terms. You must be at least 16 years old to use Daktylo.

Scores and practice

Daktylo measures speed the standard way, counting five characters as one word, and explains each figure in the app under How Speed Is Measured. Results depend on your device, your keyboard app and how you type, so they can differ from other apps, websites or tests. Daktylo is a practice tool: it is not connected to, approved by or a substitute for any typing exam, school, employer or exam board, and your results in it are not an official certificate of your speed.
