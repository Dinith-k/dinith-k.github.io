About Hilalo

A calm Hijri calendar: today's Islamic date beside the Gregorian date, the Hijri month at a glance, Ramadan, the Eids and the Islamic year with reminders, a date converter, your own Hijri dates and home-screen widgets, in seven languages. Dates follow the Umm al-Qura calendar, with an adjustment for your local moon sighting. No account and no location permission: your dates stay on your phone.
