Calculated dates

Hilalo's Hijri dates follow the Umm al-Qura calendar of Saudi Arabia, calculated in advance, moved by the adjustment you choose. In many countries and communities a month begins only once the new crescent has been sighted, so the dates there, especially the start of Ramadan and the Eids, can differ from Hilalo's by a day or two. Event dates in Hilalo are expected dates, not religious rulings. For fasting, prayer, Hajj and every act of worship, always follow the announcement of the religious authority where you live. The short descriptions of each occasion are general information, and practices vary between communities.
