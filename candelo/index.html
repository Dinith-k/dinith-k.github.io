About Candelo

A beautiful lux and foot-candle meter: a live gauge with min, max and average, a chart and light logs, from your phone's light sensor or its camera. Candelo Pro adds plant light (PPFD and daily light estimates for your plants), an exposure meter for photography, CSV export and a home-screen widget. Readings are estimates, and you can calibrate them against a meter you trust. No account and no server of ours: your readings and logs are kept on your phone.
